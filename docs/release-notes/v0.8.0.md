web888-ng's SD card image with web888d v0.11.0: **FT8, FT4 and WSPR
decoded in your browser** on the frequency you tune, and **skimmers that
need about a third less CPU**, enough for FT8 and FT4 on all of 160-6 m
at once. Test images, for owners who have their Web-888 at hand.

**Updating from v0.3.0 or later** (SSH, with an `ssh_key` in
`web888.txt`):

```sh
rauc install https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/releases/download/v0.8.0/web888-ng-v0.8.0.raucb
reboot
```

The unit keeps its settings and goes back to the previous system by
itself if the new one doesn't come up ([docs/updates.md](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/blob/v0.8.0/docs/updates.md)).
Units on v0.1.0 or v0.2.0 write this card image instead (keep a copy of
`web888.txt`).

**How to try it:** [docs/testing-the-sd-image.md](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/blob/v0.8.0/docs/testing-the-sd-image.md).

## New since v0.7.0

* **web888d v0.11.0** ([release notes](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng/releases/tag/v0.11.0)):
  * **FT8, FT4 and WSPR in the browser**: the **FT8/FT4/WSPR** button
    lists what is decoded on the frequency you tune, with the usual dial
    frequencies to pick from and a deeper search (**Deep**). Decoded in
    the browser: nothing is reported anywhere, and the unit spends
    nothing on it.
  * **Skimmers on less CPU**: a busy FT8 slot takes 2.3 s of the
    Web-888's CPU instead of 3.5 s, for the same decodes. A unit with
    nobody listening decodes FT8 on 11 bands and FT4 on 9 (160-6 m)
    with every slot done; with many listeners the quietest bands are
    shed as before. Skimmers are set in the admin pages (`[[skimmer]]`).
  * FT4 slots are no longer cut short next to FT8 bands.
  * Picking a JTTY dial on 40 m no longer leaves the receiver in LSB.
* Nothing else changes: the same bitstreams (the HF channelizer of
  web888-ng-hw v0.4.0, the VHF one of v0.5.0, HF and VHF of v0.2.0), the
  same driver and settings.

## Tested

In the emulator (web888-ng-hw `emu/tests/`):
* the image's boot through the boot ROM, both slots and `web888.txt`
  (`sd-image.sh`), with no bitstream line and with `bitstream =
  standard`;
* **the update from the published v0.7.0 image to this release's bundle**
  (`ota.sh`).

The emulator's FPGA is the standard HF one: it runs neither the
channelizer nor VHF.

web888d v0.11.0 has run on a Web-888 since 2026-11-12, under a system
chain-loaded from the stock firmware rather than from this card, with
skimmers on 20 bands and the browser's decoding tried against the unit's
own skimmer (20 m FT8: 86 messages in common, about 10 marginal ones on
either side).

## Known limits

* The channelizer (the default since v0.7.0) has not yet run from a card
  image on a board; `bitstream = standard` in `web888.txt` brings back
  the standard bitstream.
* VHF (`band = vhf`) still needs testing against real signals or a signal
  generator (see v0.7.0's notes).
* The browser's FT4 and WSPR decoding was checked on recordings, not yet
  live in a browser.
* Kiwi clients get levels in dBFS unless `[kiwi] smeter_offset_db` is
  set: the dBm calibration hasn't been measured yet.
* Updates leave `BOOT.BIN` and `u-boot.img` alone (they aren't A/B); a
  release that needs new ones will say so.
* Assumes the usual 24.576 MHz reference for the ADC clock.
* The bitstream setting in the admin pages has no effect unless
  `web888.txt` says `bitstream = standard` (`web888.txt` decides).
* Over plain HTTP the browser plays audio with about a quarter second more
  delay. The status bar links to the HTTPS address (the browser warns once
  about the unit's own certificate).
* The stock firmware's extensions and the Kiwi browser UI are not
  included.

## Files

* `web888-ng-sd-v0.8.0.img.xz`: the card image; `.img.bmap` for
  `bmaptool`
* `web888-ng-v0.8.0.raucb`: the update bundle, for units on v0.3.0 or
  later
* `SHA256SUMS`
* `web888-ng-sd-v0.8.0.spdx.json` and `.manifest`: the software in the
  image and its licences (SPDX). Sources: the recipes in this repository
  at the tag, and the upstream projects they name.
