web888-ng's SD card image with **the channelizer as the default
bitstream**, and **VHF reception (experimental)**: airband and the 2 m
band through the Web-888's VHF input, with a channelizer built for it,
and web888d v0.10.0 with narrow-band FM. Test images, for owners who have
their Web-888 at hand.

> **The channelizer is now the default.** Without a `bitstream` line in
> `web888.txt` the unit runs the channelizer: the whole band in bins, 32
> receivers, the full-band waterfall and zoomed views computed in the
> FPGA. This applies to updated units too. It has run on a Web-888 since
> 2026-10-29, but under a system chain-loaded from the stock firmware:
> **it has not yet been run from this card image on a board.** If it
> gives you trouble, `bitstream = standard` in `web888.txt` brings back
> the standard bitstream (12 receivers, the one with the most hours on
> boards) at the next boot.

> **VHF still needs testing against real signals or a signal
> generator.** It has run on a Web-888 with nothing on its VHF
> connector: the clock, the bitstream, the RF switch and the frequency
> mapping work, but no VHF station has been received with it yet.
> Levels, sensitivity, what else the VHF input lets in, and audio
> quality on real signals are unknown. Reports from units with a VHF
> antenna or a signal generator are very welcome.

**Updating from v0.3.0 or later** (SSH, with an `ssh_key` in
`web888.txt`):

```sh
rauc install https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/releases/download/v0.7.0/web888-ng-v0.7.0.raucb
reboot
```

The unit keeps its settings and goes back to the previous system by
itself if the new one doesn't come up ([docs/updates.md](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/blob/v0.7.0/docs/updates.md)).
Units on v0.1.0 or v0.2.0 write this card image instead (keep a copy of
`web888.txt`).

**How to try it:** [docs/testing-the-sd-image.md](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-os/blob/v0.7.0/docs/testing-the-sd-image.md).

## New since v0.6.0

* **The channelizer by default** (see the note at the top); `bitstream
  = standard` for the standard one.
* **`band = vhf` in `web888.txt`** (experimental): the VHF input,
  98.304-147.456 MHz by undersampling (the ADC clocked at 98.304 MHz,
  as the stock firmware's airband mode). It uses the new VHF channelizer;
  with `bitstream = standard`, the VHF bitstream with 13 receivers
  (web888-ng-hw v0.2.0's, which has not run on hardware). Back
  to HF: `band = hf` (or no line) and a reboot.
* **The VHF channelizer of web888-ng-hw v0.5.0**
  ([release notes](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng-hw/releases/tag/v0.5.0)):
  24 kHz bins, 32 receivers at 12 kS/s, the full-band waterfall and the
  zoom engine as on HF. The HF channelizer stays v0.4.0's, the one that
  has run on a board; the driver is unchanged.
* **web888d v0.10.0** ([release notes](https://github.com/jeskko/web888-ng/releases/tag/v0.10.0)):
  * VHF (`[radio] band = "vhf"`); the web UI, Kiwi clients and HPSDR
    see the real frequencies;
  * **NBFM**, a new mode for FM voice: an FM button (key f) and 12.5 kHz
    steps; Kiwi clients' `nbfm` now gets FM;
  * the phone's band picker offers the bands in range (Air and 2 m on
    VHF);
  * skimmer bands outside the receiver's range aren't run: on VHF the
    HF FT8 skimmers stop instead of reporting wrong spots.
* On HF nothing changes apart from web888d v0.10.0.

## Tested

In the emulator (web888-ng-hw `emu/tests/`):
* the image's boot through the boot ROM, both slots and `web888.txt`
  (`sd-image.sh`), with no bitstream line (the channelizer chosen) and
  with `bitstream = standard`;
* **the update from the published v0.6.0 image to this release's bundle**
  (`ota.sh`).

The emulator's FPGA is the standard HF one: it runs neither the
channelizer nor VHF.

On a Web-888 (2026-11-12): the VHF channelizer and web888d v0.10.0's
`band = "vhf"` under a system chain-loaded from the stock firmware, not
from this card, with nothing on the VHF connector: the clock confirmed
(the receivers' rate), the RF switch on VHF (the HF antenna's stations
more than 55 dB down), a flat noise floor, zoomed views; back to HF with
reception as before. **Not tried: real VHF signals, and `band = vhf`
from a card image.**

## Known limits

* VHF: see the note at the top. On VHF the HF bands' skimmers don't run.
  NBFM at 12 kS/s clips the peaks of 5 kHz deviation (25 kHz channels);
  2.5 kHz deviation fits.
* Kiwi clients get levels in dBFS unless `[kiwi] smeter_offset_db` is
  set: the dBm calibration hasn't been measured yet.
* Updates leave `BOOT.BIN` and `u-boot.img` alone (they aren't A/B); a
  release that needs new ones will say so.
* Assumes the usual 24.576 MHz reference for the ADC clock.
* The bitstream setting in the admin pages has no effect unless
  `web888.txt` says `bitstream = standard` (`web888.txt` decides).
* Over plain HTTP the browser plays audio with about a quarter second more
  delay. The status bar links to the HTTPS address (the browser warns once
  about the unit's own certificate).
* The stock firmware's extensions and the Kiwi browser UI are not
  included.
